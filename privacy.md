# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: 8 November 2026. Operator: Chenyang Xiong (熊晨阳), an individual developer based in mainland China. Privacy and abuse contact: [nesgoat624@gmail.com](mailto:nesgoat624@gmail.com).

## Who may use the service

The service is intended for people aged 16 or older. We ask for a date of birth at sign-up and store it only to determine eligibility. We do not show it to other users. If you are under 16, do not create or use an account. If you believe an under-16 account exists, contact us for removal.

## Data we process and why

- Google sign-in identifier, account email, nickname and avatar: authentication, account display and support.
- Location events and derived hexagonal grid footprints: to build your map, sync it across devices, and provide features you enable. If you enable background recording and grant iOS "Always" location permission, collection may continue when the app is not on screen or is relaunched by iOS. You can pause recording in the app and change iOS permission at any time.
- Photo library location and time metadata: only after you enable photo discovery, to find photos associated with your grid cells. Photo content is uploaded only after you select it. Uploaded thumbnails and originals, avatars, and related metadata support the photo and map features.
- Friendships, access requests, one-to-one messages, message read state, and visit activity: to provide social features and prevent repeat notifications.
- Reports and blocked users: to handle abuse, protect users, and enforce community rules.
- Technical diagnostics and device data needed to operate the app, prevent abuse and investigate failures. Some diagnostics remain on your device.

The legal basis depends on your jurisdiction and the feature: provision of the service you request, your explicit choices for optional location/photo discovery and sharing, and legitimate interests in security and abuse prevention. You may withdraw an optional choice in Settings; prior lawful processing is unaffected.

## Visibility and sharing

Sharing footprints and uploaded photos with other users is off by default. If you turn it on, people who visited the same grid cell may see your activity and photos in that cell. Friends or people you grant full access may see your full shared map. You may switch sharing off; newly requested views will be denied. People who already downloaded or saw content may retain copies. Blocking a user prevents new access through the app. Do not upload photos of others without a lawful basis or permission where required.

## Providers and international transfers

Supabase hosts account, social and photo data in its Tokyo region. Google provides sign-in and Mapbox provides map tiles and related map services; these providers may process requests and technical data in other jurisdictions under their own terms. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. We will review transfer mechanisms required for each launch country before offering the service there.

## Storage, security and retention

Newly uploaded photos are held in private storage and delivered through short-lived links after access checks. Photos uploaded before the privacy migration may still be reachable through their old public URLs while the legacy client transition is completed. You can delete your account and its photos at any time. We use access rules and limited permissions, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Account, footprint, photo and chat data are retained while your account is active. Account deletion removes the account, uploaded media and associated records, except information we must retain under applicable law. Temporary backups and provider logs may remain for a limited period under provider schedules. Reports are reviewed for abuse; contact us if you need details about a particular retention obligation.

## Your choices and rights

In the app, you can pause location recording, disable photo discovery and sharing, block users, export your account records and photo download links, or permanently delete the account. Exported photo links expire after 24 hours; request a new export if needed. You may also email us to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or to object, subject to applicable law. We will respond within the legally required period.

## Changes and contact

We will post material changes here and update the effective date. Contact [nesgoat624@gmail.com](mailto:nesgoat624@gmail.com) for privacy requests or complaints. You may also complain to your local data protection authority where applicable.
