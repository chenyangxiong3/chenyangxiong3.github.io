# Community Rules / 社区规则

Effective date: 8 November 2026. This service is for users aged 16 and above.

Do not upload or send harassment, threats, hateful content, sexual exploitation material, non-consensual intimate images, doxxing, spam, unlawful content, or photos that violate another person's privacy, portrait rights or intellectual property rights. Do not use location features to track or intimidate another person. Respect restrictions on photography and mapping in the place where you are located.

Use the in-app Report action on a user, photo or message. You may also block another user; blocking stops new app access between both accounts. Reports are reviewed by the operator, 熊晨阳, at [nesgoat624@gmail.com](mailto:nesgoat624@gmail.com). We may remove content, restrict accounts and contact authorities where legally required. For urgent physical danger, contact local emergency services first.

禁止骚扰、威胁、仇恨、性剥削内容、未经同意的私密影像、公开他人敏感信息、垃圾信息及违法内容。不得通过定位功能跟踪或恐吓他人；拍摄和上传内容须尊重当地法律及他人的肖像、隐私和知识产权。可在应用内举报用户、照片或消息并屏蔽用户，也可发邮件至上述地址。运营者会处理举报并视情况移除内容或限制账号。
