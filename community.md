# Community Rules / 社区规则

Effective date: 20 November 2026. This service generally requires age 16 or older, or a confirmed age of 18 or older when Apple’s regional and account signals require age assurance.

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Do not upload or send harassment, threats, hateful content, sexual exploitation material, non-consensual intimate images, doxxing, spam, unlawful content, or photos that violate another person's privacy, portrait rights or intellectual property rights. Do not use location features to track or intimidate another person. Respect restrictions on photography and mapping in the place where you are located.

Use the in-app Report action on a user, photo or message. You may also block another user; blocking stops new app access between both accounts. Reports are reviewed by the operator, 熊晨阳, at [nesgoat624@gmail.com](mailto:nesgoat624@gmail.com). For nonconsensual intimate images or digitally forged intimate depictions (no Yonprint account required), use our dedicated [48-Hour Intimate Imagery Removal Portal](takedown.html). We may remove content, restrict accounts and contact authorities where legally required. For urgent physical danger, contact local emergency services first.
